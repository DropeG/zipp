# Mercado Libre Quality Catalog

Last normative review: 2026-09-03. Review monthly and immediately after an
unknown Mercado Libre warning, rejection or requirement. Scheduled product
runs use this local catalog; they do not browse the web every two hours.

## Achievable controls

- Category describes the product type and primary function.
- Initial title preserves Shopify identity, is concise and contains no
  forbidden store/contact trace. Alternative titles require a real rejection.
- Required and recommended characteristics are filled when Shopify proves them.
- Unknown values remain unknown; `No aplica` means the characteristic truly
  does not apply.
- GTIN is supplied when Shopify has a real barcode; otherwise use only the
  category/API-supported missing-identifier mechanism.
- Variants, price and stock exactly reflect currently sellable Shopify options.
- Gallery shows only the sold product, uses faithful Shopify evidence, contains
  no Zipp/store/contact marks, and meets category-specific cover rules.
- Description is clear, source-faithful and free of store/contact content.
- Mercado Envíos and free shipping are requested when Meli permits them.
- The final item is repeatedly clean-active and expected attributes persist.

## Commercial recommendations to record, not force

- Increase stock.
- Change the Shopify price to compete or fund shipping.
- Add discounts or promotions.
- Buy Premium exposure or interest-free installments.
- Adopt wholesale or other paid commercial commitments.

These recommendations never authorize a mutation. Record each surfaced but
unapplied objective and its reason in the run's quality record.

## Category-sensitive photo guidance

Do not treat one photo rule as universal. Current official guidance permits
contextual backgrounds in many categories while still requiring white
backgrounds in Technology, Beauty, Health and Supermarket. Prefer an existing
eligible Shopify cover. Perform controlled background removal only after an
actual Meli objection and only if the product remains pixel-faithful.
