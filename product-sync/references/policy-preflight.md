# Mercado Libre Policy Preflight

Read this reference only for products with policy hints or other sensitivity.
Policies change, so confirm the current official page before a live attempt.

Official starting points:

- Publication policies: https://www.mercadolibre.cl/seguro_publicacion.html
- Prohibited products: https://www.mercadolibre.cl/ayuda/5200
- Intellectual property: https://www.mercadolibre.cl/seguro_violppi.html
- Brand Protection: https://www.mercadolibre.cl/brandprotection/enforcement
- Lithium batteries and perfumes:
  https://vendedores.mercadolibre.cl/nota/perfumes-y-baterias-de-litio-como-identificar-y-enviar-estos-productos

Record the page checked and the relevant conclusion in the run notes. A stale
historical exception is not current policy evidence.

## Contact and buyer-facing content

Remove domains, URLs, emails, phone numbers, WhatsApp/WSP, social networks,
addresses and contact instructions from titles, descriptions, photos where
avoidable, brand, model, MPN and compatibility attributes. If an original photo
contains prohibited contact information, do not edit it in this workflow; omit
it if other Shopify images suffice or block the product.

Compatibility wording must not imply manufacture, authorization or official
status by Apple, Samsung, MagSafe or another brand unless the source proves it.
Do not publish apparent replicas or counterfeit products.

## Sensitive classes

- Normal accessory: proceed with standard validation.
- Shipping-sensitive: batteries, lithium, power banks, perfumes, aerosols,
  flammable liquids, pressurized products and chemicals. Check current shipping
  eligibility; do not decide from keywords alone.
- Policy-sensitive: medical or therapeutic claims, regulated health products,
  weapons, surveillance misuse, restricted substances, adult/illegal content,
  services, accounts, keys and digital downloads. Stop unless current official
  policy clearly permits the exact product.
- Brand/IP-sensitive: mismatched visible brand, replicas, third-party logos or
  unauthorized-origin claims. Use truthful visible branding or stop.

## Images

Use original Shopify images only. Mercado Libre may prefer a clean white cover,
but that preference is not permission to edit the product photo. First reorder
the existing source images. If none passes moderation, record the image blocker.

If an original photo contains prohibited contact information or Zipp/store
branding, omit it. Do not erase foreground logos or text under the remediation
exception.

Background removal is allowed only after a real Meli cover objection, only for
the background, and with provenance and pixel-fidelity checks. It never permits
removing a logo or text printed on the product.
